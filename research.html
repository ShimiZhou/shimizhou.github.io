Research

Focus Areas & Projects

Studying collaboration, knowledge production, and leadership across networks, cities, and AI, with methods from social network analysis, statistics, and machine learning.

Inclusive & AI-Ready Smart Cities

Examining how smart city technologies can equitably serve all residents, including people with disabilities, and how municipalities build genuine AI readiness. Work spans a systematic literature review (EJIS) and a maturity model and assessment toolkit to help city managers move toward responsible AI deployment in urban governance.

Smart CitiesAccessibilityAI MaturityValue Co-creationGovernance

Gender Equity in Academic Networks

Using social network analysis to study collaboration patterns, homophily, and research impact among IS scholars. Work examines how gender shapes co-authorship, institutional ties, and career outcomes in the IS academic community.

Gender EquitySocial Network AnalysisHomophilyBibliometrics

AI Thought Leadership & Decision-Making

Analyzing leadership discourse around generative AI using text analytics and NLP. Current work examines how executives and academics frame AI decision-making, and what this reveals about power, authority, and responsibility.

Generative AILeadership DiscourseNLPText Analytics
Ongoing — stay tuned

Societal Impact of Generative AI

A newer direction exploring the broader societal consequences of generative AI, including effects on equity, labor, knowledge production, and social participation. Projects are underway, with more to come.

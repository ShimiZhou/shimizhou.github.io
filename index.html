About

Background & Interests

I am an Assistant Professor of Information Systems (IS) at the Bryan School of Business and Economics, University of North Carolina at Greensboro (UNCG). I received my Ph.D. in Business Administration (Information Systems) from Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) in May 2024, where I also earned my M.S. in Information Technology.

My research explores the intersection of information technology (IT), data science, and social inclusion. I am particularly interested in how technology shapes equitable participation in academia, organizations, and society, across emerging areas such as gender equity in academic and professional networks, thought leadership in AI, and the role of data justice in inclusive technology.

Technology does not simply mirror society — it actively constructs it. Understanding how IS shapes who gets to collaborate, lead, and belong is both a scholarly and a moral imperative.

Before joining UNCG, I worked as an Associate Data Scientist in Advanced Analytics & AI at John Hancock | Manulife in Boston, which brings an industry lens to my academic work. I was also a Research Assistant on the NSF ADVANCE ImPACT IT project, focused on increasing the participation and advancement of women in information technology.
